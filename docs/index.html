About

Decoding biology
through computation

I'm a computational scientist with 5+ years of experience developing machine learning, Bayesian statistical models, and reproducible data pipelines for multimodal biomedical data. My PhD focuses on characterising the spatial tissue microenvironment in Inflammatory Bowel Disease using Visium spatial transcriptomics, computational pathology, and clinical outcome modelling.

Currently a visiting PhD student at the Wellcome Sanger Institute (Bayraktar group, Hinxton, UK), where I'm designing multimodal deep learning frameworks integrating GeoMx RNA profiles with H&E histology features to characterise crypt-level heterogeneity in IBD.

I believe in open, reproducible science — most of my work ships as open-source tools and public pipelines.
