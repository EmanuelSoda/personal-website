Projects & Tools

I develop computational methods and open-source software for analysing large-scale multimodal biomedical datasets. My focus is on spatial transcriptomics, computational pathology, and clinical outcome modelling in IBD.

Designing multimodal deep learning frameworks integrating GeoMx Digital Spatial Profiling RNA profiles and H&E histology features to characterise crypt-level transcriptional and morphological heterogeneity across Inflammatory Bowel Disease and healthy control patients.

IBD tissue microenvironment — spatial transcriptomics
